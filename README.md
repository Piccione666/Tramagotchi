# Tramagotchi

Your Vienna tram countdown.

## First start

1. Plug it in.
2. If the screen says **Setup WiFi**: on your phone, join the WiFi **Tramagotchi**. A setup page opens (if not, go to **<http://192.168.4.1>**).
3. Pick your WiFi, enter the password, tap **Save and restart**.

## Choose your stops

1. On a phone or computer in the same WiFi, open **<http://tramagotchi.local>**.
2. Choose a line, pick your stop, tick the direction, tap **Add**.
3. Repeat for up to 5 stops, then tap **Save**.

Tip: in the list you can shorten the direction text. That's the label shown on the screen.
To remove stops, delete their lines in the list and tap **Save**.
The list of lines and stops updates itself. To check right away, tap **Update stop data**.

## The screen

- **Big number**: minutes until the next departure. **Small number**: the one after.
- **Blinking squares**: departing now (or loading).
- **>_<**: nothing running right now, or no internet.
- A face popping up now and then is just saying hi.

## Button

- **Press**: next stop. **Hold**: face mode on/off.

## New WiFi?

Plug it in at the new place. The **Tramagotchi** WiFi appears again; set it up as above.
